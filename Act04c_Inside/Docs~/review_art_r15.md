# Art review r15 · Act 4c "门内" (independent, 2026-10-24)
I looked at all 10 r15 frames next to 14_fray, plus the 196.0-198.1 strip and 2-3x crops. Luma is Rec.709 from the PNGs. The bus, frame and pillar are not judged. The grade_state is identical to r14. The dot core is 238-240 at (425,236) everywhere. No r15 stranger results exist yet, so the cloth read below is my own judgement.

## Beauty / cloth read
- **197 is the best frame so far, and the first one that reads as cloth.** One tongue hangs from the top edge (x 290-360) with a vertical pleat seam and a stepped tip. In the strip it grows smoothly with approach (mean Δ 5-6 luma per 0.3 s) and does not pop. Its rim is still a soft ~2 px glow all round, so a cold viewer could still call it an icicle.
- **226 side has improved.** The rim is thinner and the lumps are gone, so it reads as a hanging strip more than an ice column. The x ≈ 480 line is still there (S2).
- **228-246.4** are unchanged in substance and still calm.

## Blockers
- **B5, faces (pareidolia).** 181: the dim tongue at x 20-175, y 150-360 is a clean human head profile, with brow, nose, chin and neck. 200: the bright tongue at x 40-180, y 0-140 reads as a skull: two dark pleat hollows are the eyes, the step is the jaw, and its mirror in the side window (x 790-900) repeats it. This is worse than rock. Tongue silhouettes must not have a single concave notch on one side near mid-height. Taper them or fray them. Make the pleat shading run full length, with no blob-shaped darks.
- **B6, pixel stairs on the near sheets (205).** Both near sheets now have a regular sawtooth edge: the left at x 0-170, y 200-450, and the right at x 820-880, full height. They have equal step pitch and horizontal banding, which reads as stacked slate, crystal or stairs. Weave tears need long *uneven* runs, so the step spacing should vary by at least 3:1 and most of the length should be straight.
- **B7, 205 arch tongue.** The only tongue (x 265-300, y 205-250) is a short, square-cornered notch, which reads as crenellation or a cave mouth, not cloth. B4 from r14 is still open: the far-lip fringe is barely back.
- **B8, unannounced dot-halo dim.** The core is unchanged, but the mean of the 30 px ring fell from 132→86 (197), 135→92 (205) and 188→165 (240), even though the grade is the same. Find the cause and restore it, or justify it. The dot must not lose presence.

## Still not fixed
- **218.75** still has the internal crack network plus a horizontal spike (x 160-230, y 210) and a stalactite top (x 400-560). It reads as shattered glass or ice. The tears inside the sheet should follow the weave as well, or be removed.
- **181** keeps a thick, bright bevel on the left near sheet. The N4 curls are still there (x 60-140, y 90-130), and so are the floaters.
